# Brand

## Names

- Product: **Good Standing**. Everything a reader sees uses it.
- Trading: **Good Standing Software**, never with Ltd.
- Legal: **Good Standing Software Ltd** (17423889).
- Never write that the company trades as Good Standing.

## Titles

- Home is brand first: `Good Standing | software for UK membership bodies`. Every other page is topic first: `Privacy notice | Good Standing`, `Thanks | Good Standing`.
- `|` separates title parts and appears nowhere else in a title.
- `<title>`, `og:title`, `twitter:title`, and any JSON-LD `WebPage` `name` or `headline` match.
- The `h1` names the same topic as the title, and the meta description describes the page (privacy is a notice, not a pitch).

## Voice

- Public pages speak as **we**: approachable, professional, intriguing; never matey or salesy.
- Research comes before product. There is nothing to buy and no launch call to action.
- Never write "CPD". Say continuing education, fees, membership, or good standing.
- The privacy page is an ICO-style notice. Keep its legal structure; never rewrite it for tone or spacing.

## Links

- Internal directory links keep a trailing slash (`privacy/`, `../`).
- External links open with `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- Survey links carry `?ref=` naming where they sit: `homepage`, `footer`.

Tally form content is edited in Tally, never from here.
